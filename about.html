Our Story

Where passion for food and community come together.

Founded with Love

Namit's Café was born in 2020 out of a love for great food, warm conversations, and the joy of bringing people together. Our founder, Namit Khatiwada, dreamed of creating a space where every visitor feels at home—whether they're here for a quick coffee, a hearty meal, or a cozy chat with friends.

Located in the heart of Kathmandu, our café blends traditional Nepali hospitality with modern culinary innovation. We take pride in sourcing the freshest local ingredients and crafting dishes that celebrate both global and local flavors.

Our Mission

To serve delicious, high-quality food and beverages that nourish the body and soul. We strive to create a welcoming environment where everyone feels valued and inspired.

Our Vision

To be the most beloved café in Kathmandu, known for our exceptional service, innovative menu, and commitment to sustainability and community.
